Contribute to IQS Connect

This site is a living record of the IQS competition — and the teams make it what it is. If your team's profile is missing a bio, photos, or social links, here's how to get it updated.

1
Create an account
Sign up for a free account using your school email. You'll receive a verification email to confirm your address before you can log in.
Sign Up
2
Find your team and request access
Browse the teams list and open your school's team page. From there you can request to be added as a team member. A current team member or admin will need to approve your request.
Browse Teams
3
Update your team profile
Once you're a member, you can edit your team's profile — add a bio, links to your teams website, Instagram, YouTube, and more. Keep it current so fans and judges can learn about your program.
4
Add photos
Photos can be uploaded directly from event pages and run detail pages. Visit the event your team competed in, open your team's entry for that event, and use the upload button to add photos from the competition.
Browse Events
5
Update your tractor
Each tractor has its own profile page with specs and build details. If your tractor's info is incomplete or out of date, you can edit it from the tractor's detail page while logged in as a team member.
Browse Tractors
Questions or issues? If you're having trouble getting access or something isn't working, reach out to us at asabeiqswebsite@gmail.com.
